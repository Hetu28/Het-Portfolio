ABOUT — PORTRAIT OF A FILMMAKER

I chase the quiet frames that feel loud in memory.

I’m Het — a filmmaker working across narrative shorts, commercials, documentaries and music videos. My work sits at the intersection of restraint and emotion.

As a director and DOP I shape light, blocking and rhythm. As an editor I sculpt time. The goal is always the same: give an audience a frame they can’t forget.
